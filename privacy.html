Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Color Me Fun & Notes (the "app"), made by Smart Choice Tampa LLC ("we", "us"), handles information.

The short version: Color Me Fun & Notes does not collect any information about you or your child. There are no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, no analytics and no tracking. Drawings stay on your iPad.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. In the App Store, the app is listed as Data Not Collected.

Drawings and pictures

Everything your child creates — drawings, notes, favorites, profiles and settings — is saved only on your iPad, inside the app. We never see it.

Camera, photos and speech

If a grown-up chooses Add Your Own Page (the app asks a grown-up question first), the app asks for permission to use the camera or to pick a photo or file. The picture is used only to make a page inside the app and stays on your iPad. It is never uploaded to us. The app saves a finished picture or video to Photos only when a grown-up answers the grown-up question and taps Save, and it never looks through your photo library on its own.

Reading a page's name aloud uses the iPad's own voice. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is not used.

Saving, sharing and printing

Save, Share and Print ask a grown-up question first. When you save a drawing to Photos, share it, print it, or make a backup, you choose where it goes using Apple's standard screens (for example your Photos library, Files or iCloud Drive). The app does not send it anywhere else.

Feedback and diagnostics

The feedback form only prepares an e-mail in your Mail app. Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and you decide whether to add the app version and iPad model.

The app keeps a short private diary of what it was doing (for example "opened a page" or "save failed") and Apple's own crash reports, on your iPad only. They contain no drawings, names or titles. If the app closes unexpectedly, a grown-up may choose to e-mail them to us; nothing is sent unless they press Send in Mail.

If you allow your iPad to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. They contain nothing about you.

Page pack downloads

Some coloring pages come in extra packs that download from Apple's servers, the same way apps download from the App Store. We do not receive any information about these downloads. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children and families. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13. There are no links out of the app, no chat and no in-app advertising. Settings, the Grown-ups area and Add Your Own Page are protected by a grown-up question. A grown-up can switch the grown-up question off in the Grown-ups area. It is on unless they do. Profile names you type stay on this iPad and in backups you make.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@smartchoiceapps.com. If you e-mail us, we use your address only to reply.
